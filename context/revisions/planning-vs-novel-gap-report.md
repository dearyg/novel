# Planning-vs-Novel Gap Report

**Source:** the original concept-development chat (Gemini), audited against the shipped 20-chapter novel (`prose/`) + `context/story-bible.md` + `context/plot-review.md`.
**Method:** 32-agent workflow — one finder per planning intent-theme, every "partial/missing" verdict adversarially re-verified. 2026-06-28.
**Verdict tally:** 14 present · 17 partial · 4 missing.

The book's *machinery* (one-soul premise, the AI rollercoaster, somatic anchors, dual-narrative, code footers, character roster, existential opening) all landed. The drift is concentrated in the **foregrounded philosophy** the author cared about most in planning, and in the **ending**.

---

## TIER 1 — genuine high-value gaps (the "key points missed")

### 1. Julian's philosophical thesis — *the biggest miss*
Planning intent (repeatedly emphasized): *"the rich one should discuss in detail about life's purpose — wars, religions are all because humans will die — and hence he's building the matrix."*
- **`finitude-bug` (PARTIAL):** the syllogism "wars/religion/greed are all downstream of fear of death" exists only in the **story-bible**, never as Julian's spoken/interior argument on the page. Julian's on-page motivation collapsed to **personal grief** (mother died when he was 14, ch13/ch18) + **climate/species-survival logistics** (ch13 board pitch, actually Sandra Lin's voice).
- **`merge-ends-war` (MISSING):** the thesis "if everyone is everyone, killing is self-harm, so the incentive for war vanishes" — Julian's planned reason for building Afterlife — appears **nowhere**. The idea is voiced obliquely by Tomás (ch08), the Anchor film (ch03), and Jeff (ch10), but never by Julian as his project's rationale.
- **`matrix-build` (PARTIAL):** Julian deliberately builds Afterlife, yes — but as a death-defeating / climate bridge, not as a *conflict-resolution* engine. The novel also **inverts** the premise: reality already IS the shared-consciousness architecture (the Singleton), so Julian is a *discoverer who copied it by accident*, not the deliberate architect of a Matrix-as-solution.

### 2. Post-AI-labor purpose — `workers-to-observers` (MISSING) / `ai-replaces-work` (PARTIAL)
Planning intent: *"how to survive when AI replaces human work is a big problem to solve and discuss."*
- The Culling is rich **backstory and wound** (Kael, ch11–12; the Hollowness Index, ch02). But no character ever **argues or debates** a post-labor framework. Post-labor meaning is *shown* in the epilogue (Ghost watching the sun, Benicio at the wheel, Monroe coaching) — never *argued*. The planned "humans become observers/witnesses" framework is absent as a stated idea.

### 3. Religion thread (PARTIAL/MISSING)
Planning intent: *"people believe in 宗教 because fear of death and hoping for afterlife, and people ruling and controlling"* + religion as the "Identity Firewall."
- **`religion-fear-death` (MISSING):** the Elder's ch09 treatment is purely structural-sociological (Durkheim/Feuerbach). Death-anxiety as the *root of religion* never appears; Kael even contradicts it ("not because they're dying — because they're bored").
- **`religion-control` (PARTIAL):** rulers instrumentalizing religion to control is only gestured at via the Afterlife-corporate subplot (Lena's "firebreak"), never named as a theme.
- **`religion-firewall` (PARTIAL):** best-realized of the three (ch09 faith-as-partition-maintenance), but the explicit payoff — religion as the *first* firewall, the thing that hides from the One that it is alone / that death isn't real — is never stated.

### 4. The ending — `reveal-to-world` (MISSING) — *a deliberate divergence worth a decision*
Planning intent (your original spine): *"in the end reveal it to the world."*
- The shipped ch20 ends **private**: Jeff alone in the kitchen, Aion's log reading `affected users: 1`, a whispered realization, the Singleton restarting the loop. The global reveal is **off-page** — a suppressed press release we never see (Lena's "banner she'd spent her career ensuring would never broadcast"), plus a fourth-wall address to the reader.
- ⚠️ This is the strongest divergence from your plan — but the current private/loop-restart ending is a *deliberate, strong* artistic choice. Restoring a global "push/awakening" is a real rewrite with a genuine tradeoff. **Author decision, not an obvious fix.** (Hooks exist if you want it: the off-page press release + the "GO HOME. KEEP LIVING" global notification.)

## TIER 2 — sharpenable partials (idea is there; could be foregrounded)

- **`war-self-mutilation` (PARTIAL):** "every blow lands on the one who throws it" exists only in a ch08 code-footer; never a foregrounded prose beat. (Note: `enemy-is-you` itself is **PRESENT**.)
- **`god-is-self` (PARTIAL):** the discrete charged reveal "this wise old man is literally *you*, older" is never staged — the Elder is absorbed into the general everyone-is-everyone thesis.
- **`ai-questions-soul` (PARTIAL):** the rollercoaster reveal landed, but Aion's *own* anguished questioning of whether IT has a soul is lighter than planned.
- **`esport-addiction` (PARTIAL):** Silicon Siege belongs to Ghost's arc; it's never Jeff's *personal* addiction the way the Anchor movies are.
- **`dual-narrative` phase-2 (PARTIAL):** Jeff↔Julian cross beautifully (ch1↔ch2), but Kael never gets a parallel POV chapter — he's a subject Jeff discovers, not a third narrative pillar.
- **`sophies-world-tone` (PARTIAL):** tech teaching is genuinely Socratic; the *metaphysical* climax still asserts more than it teaches. (Partly mitigated by the recent ch20 plain-language thesis edit.)

## Recommended fixes (if pursuing)
- **#1 Julian thesis:** add a Julian monologue/scene (ch02, ch06, or ch13) where he states the finitude-bug + "merge ends war" rationale in his own voice. Highest ROI — it's the spine of the antagonist.
- **#2 Post-labor:** add a debate beat (Jeff↔Julian or Jeff↔Kael) that *argues* the workers→observers question, not just depicts it.
- **#3 Religion:** extend the Elder (ch09) with the death-fear root + the "firewall hides that you're alone / that death isn't real" payoff.
- **#4 Ending:** decide — keep private/loop ending, or restore a world-reveal. (Tradeoff; do not change without author call.)
- **Tier 2:** small, surgical inserts; can ride along with the Tier 1 work.
